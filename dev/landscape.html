Therefore · The spine

Five questions separate two decks that use the same word.

01
What do you mean by outcome?
Reach, attention, a click, an attributed conversion, a sale, an incremental sale.
02
How do you know it happened?
Who observed it, and on whose numbers.
03
How do you know advertising caused it?
Attributed, or measured against a control.
04
Why should I expect it for me?
How far the result travels to another buyer.
05
What do you put at risk?
What the seller owes if the number is missed.

Each question is answered by a different kind of evidence. A strong answer to one is not an answer to the next.

Question 02 · how you know it happened

There is plenty of evidence. “Only 28 have proof” was the wrong reading.

Independent provenance
28

Of those 28
26

still sit at the second of five transfer levels. Two reach the third. None goes higher.

The distinction

Independent is not the same as repeatable

A commissioned study can establish what happened without establishing what happens next, for someone else.

Third-party provenance, causal authority and transfer are three separate questions. Collapsing them into one proof ladder is what made the first read too tidy.

But
Question 03 · attribution or causality

Attribution says it was counted. Incrementality says it would not have happened anyway.

Attributed

The result is assigned to the ad

Platform reporting, matched panels, modeled conversions. Abundant across the landscape, and the basis of most named-client numbers.

Incremental · 41 claims

The result is compared with a control

Geo holdouts, ghost bidding, matched markets. Rare on public pages, and almost always inside one client's test.

Both are legitimate. They answer different questions, and only one of them survives the CFO asking what the advertising changed.

The candidates split the same way. Samba TV's incremental CPM is a genuine mechanism over incremental reach. MiQ's “Guaranteed Performance” does not disclose a threshold or a remedy on the page. Across 536 arguments assembled from the same claims. Nothing collapsed. What the audit found is the step where meaning enters: a named client becomes a general performance promise, an attributed result becomes a causal one, a capability becomes a claim of superiority.

Compression is inevitable. Loss of reasoning is not.

Therefore

The open ground, stated at the size the evidence allows.

Not found in the public material

One configuration appears nowhere

A named business outcome, measured against an independent control, with a stated remedy when it is missed. Parts of it exist separately. The whole does not appear on any captured page.

What that is worth

A question, not a claim

Whether buyers want to pay for that configuration, and whether a seller can carry the risk at scale, is not something a read of public pages can settle. It is the next thing worth testing with buyers.

The honest version of the open corner is smaller than the first one, and harder to argue with.

Where it lands

Ask the five questions. The differences appear without anyone being accused of anything.

What do you mean by outcome? How do you know it happened? How do you know advertising caused it? Why should I expect it for me? What do you put at risk if it does not happen? Every seller can answer. Few can answer all five at the same altitude, and that is the map.
