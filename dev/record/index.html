Five things the record will not support

Each one is available from the figures above. None survives what was captured.

  1. 01
    The market has no proof.
    It has plenty. The question is what each number shows, and how far it travels.
  2. 02
    Vague language means weak evidence.
    Walled gardens use the plainest language of anyone and show a method on none of their claims.
  3. 03
    Outcome sellers are more honest.
    They show their method twice as often. Honesty was not measured.
  4. 04
    No seller puts a fee at risk.
    Sellers have guaranteed a business result with a stated remedy at least five times since 2011.
  5. 05
    The gap is the opportunity.
    An absence on a web page is not proof of demand.
