But · the difficulty

Which is why the market is hard to read, and harder to measure.

One word, six objects

No shared unit

A buyer comparing two decks has no common denominator. Production hours and incremental sales arrive under the same heading.

Occurrence, attribution, causality

Three different statements

That it happened, that it was credited to the ad, and that the ad caused it are distinct. Pages rarely say which one is on offer.

The proposition arrives first

230 of 536 arguments

depend on evidence found elsewhere on the same site. The claim often appears before the evidence that supports it.

The conditions are unstated

No period, base or population

Most figures arrive without the window they were measured over or the comparison they were measured against, so nothing can be restaged.

Therefore · the method

So we read every page the same way, then had the evidence adjudicated.

Two instruments, two jobs, run in two rounds. The Throughline composes the account of the market. MAVEN adjudicates it — first the claims and the arguments, then every sector conclusion this deck draws from them. Neither ranks sellers.

Promise to Proof.6
Calidescope · Deep dive
The instrument

Five questions do the sorting.

01
What do you mean by outcome?
The construct: reach, attention, an asset, a click, an attributed conversion, an incremental sale.
02
How do you know it happened?
Who observed it, on whose data, over what period.
03
How do you know advertising caused it?
Attributed, or measured against a control.
04
Why should I expect it for me?
Whether the conditions for recurrence are stated at all.
05
What do you put at risk?
What the seller owes if the number is missed.

Most sellers can answer one or two. The map is which ones they can answer at the same time.

The five questions are MAVEN's, drawn from its round-one findings package.

Promise to Proof.9
Calidescope · Deep dive
The principle

Keep the finding.
Bound the verb.

MAVEN's summary of what round two found, and the rule this version is written to. The facts underneath the market account survived the audit intact. What did not survive was a handful of words — nobody, nothing, every, least, does not travel, unclaimed, expect — where authority leaked as the evidence was compressed from claims to sectors to market patterns.

Compression is not the error. Compression is the job. The error is letting the boundary fall off on the way up.

What it adds up to

Five patterns that run across the whole captured market.

01

The proposition arrives before the proof

230 of 536 arguments depend on evidence found elsewhere on the same site.

Holds · bounded · 'Elsewhere on the same site' is not synonymous with 'case study,' and off-home-page evidence does not always fully establish the proposition.

02

Closer to the sale, closer to the seller's own data

Retail media, clouds and outcome sellers publish the richest figures and read them from systems they own. A pattern read across the sectors, not a measured law.

Partially holds · The directional pattern is interpretive: 'distance from sale' and seller-controlled data were not coded as a market-wide measured relationship.

03

The adjudicators are lightly adjudicated

Measurement and verification carry three third-party figures between them across 95 outcome claims.

Holds · bounded · The 3/95 count holds. 'Least' is not literally established because other sectors, including Retail Media, have zero third-party outcome claims.

04

Operational outcomes stand in for market outcomes

In creative, assets and hours are counted where media results would be — 53 of 66 outcome claims carry a figure, almost none a media result. In workflow it is pipeline and process time.

Partially holds · Creative strongly supports production outcomes; Workflow is primarily pipeline/process time, not production.

05

The public promise stops before the remedy

Across 2,213 claims and 627 pages, no captured page states a remedy tied to a result. Eight guarantees exist and all cover something upstream of the sale.

Partially holds · No captured page states a remedy tied to a result. The record cannot establish that no seller's actual fee moves because private contracts and many pricing surfaces are unseen.

The open ground

One configuration appears nowhere in the captured market.

Present, separately

A named outcome

Retail media, outcome sellers and clouds all name a business event and count it.

Present, separately

An independent control

Geo holdouts, ghost bidding and matched markets appear on seven of the twenty outcome sellers' pages.

Not observed anywhere

A stated remedy

No captured page says what the seller owes when the named outcome, measured against that control, is missed.

Stated at the size the evidence allows: the three parts exist in the market and never appear together on a page we captured. Whether buyers would pay for the combination, and whether a seller can carry the risk, is the next thing to test with buyers rather than a claim this read can make.

MAVEN: this slide holds as written. It stays at exactly the size the evidence permits and correctly leaves buyer demand and seller risk capacity as things to test rather than findings.

The boundary

What this read does not establish.

01
The market has no proof.It holds substantial evidence. The question is what each piece establishes.
02
Only the claims with third-party evidence are proven.Independent provenance is not a synonym for proof, and it is not transfer.
03
Only two companies guarantee a business outcome.A market-wide count needs a denominator a public crawl does not have.
04
No seller puts a fee at risk.Private contracts, make-goods and negotiated terms are not public. Nothing here reads them.
05
The missing configuration is the opportunity.An absence in public material is not evidence of demand.

Where it lands

Ask the five questions. The market sorts itself.

What do you mean by outcome? How do you know it happened? How do you know advertising caused it? Why should I expect it for me? What do you put at risk if it does not happen? Nobody is accused of anything, and the differences between two decks that use the same word become visible in about a minute.

The instrument that makes this publishable is the second one. A market account is a compression, and compression is where authority leaks. Keep the finding; bound the verb.
